Features

Everything around the teaching itself

Built by a drum teacher for real-world lessons, then extended to all instruments. Anything you don’t need can be switched off in the settings.

Lesson notes

What you practised, what the homework is, who was there. Next time, your last note is already waiting.

Curriculum per student

The levels of the Swiss music school curriculum for 28 instruments (in German). Tick off what’s mastered and see what comes next.

Sheet music with annotations

Your PDFs in the music viewer. Pen and highlighter marks are saved per student and per book.

Week and calendar

Holidays, recitals, concerts and birthdays. The week view shows what’s coming up and who comes when.

Lesson scheduling

Families enter their availability via a link. Klassenbuch proposes a timetable.

Invoices

Bill private lessons: the invoice is created from the lessons you recorded, with your payment slip.

Scheduling

Your timetable, without twenty messages back and forth

  1. Send a link. Each family gets a personal link. No account needed.
  2. Enter times. On their phone, families tap what suits them well, what works if need be and what doesn’t. They can’t see other families’ entries. The page is available in English too.
  3. Review the proposal. Klassenbuch places the students with the fewest options first and shows where it gets tight. You adjust, confirm and let each family know their time.

Security

What you note about children stays with you

Your entries are encrypted on your device before they leave it. The server only stores data nobody can read, not even the operator.

End-to-end encrypted

The key is derived from your password and never leaves your device.

On all your devices

iPad in the lesson, laptop at home, phone on the go. Always up to date.

Works offline

Keep writing without a connection. Everything syncs once you’re back online.

Recoverable

Forgot your password? Your recovery key lets you set a new one. Download backups at any time.

Get started

Set up in five minutes

Create an account

With your name, email and a password. During the test phase you need an invitation code. Ask me for one: adi.popp@hotmail.com

Add it to your device

Choose “Add to Home Screen” in your browser. Klassenbuch then opens like an app.

Add your students

Enter instrument and lesson time, choose a curriculum and off you go.
